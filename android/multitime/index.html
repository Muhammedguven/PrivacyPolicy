1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how MultiTime - World Clock Tile, developed and published by Kolapps, handles user information. The application is designed to help users track multiple world clocks and display time zones through Wear OS tiles and companion phone functionality.

Google Play Store page: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kolapps.multitime

2. Information We Collect

MultiTime does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information. The app does not require user registration or account creation.

The application may allow users to configure preferred cities and display settings. These preferences are stored locally on the user's device solely for app functionality. This information is not transmitted to Kolapps or any external servers.

3. Wear OS Communication

The companion phone application may communicate with the user's Wear OS device to synchronize app configurations and selected world clock settings. This communication occurs directly between the user's devices and is used only to provide the core functionality of the application.

No personal information is collected or transmitted during this synchronization process.

4. Third-Party Services

MultiTime does not use advertising networks or analytics services that collect personal user information.

If future versions of the application integrate additional third-party services, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

5. Data Sharing

Since MultiTime does not collect personal information, no user data is sold, shared, rented, or disclosed to third parties.

6. Data Security

Because the application does not process personal information through external servers, the privacy risks associated with data transmission are minimized. User preferences remain under the control of the device owner.

7. Children's Privacy

MultiTime is not directed toward children under the age of 13. The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
