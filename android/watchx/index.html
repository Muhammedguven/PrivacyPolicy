1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how WatchX, developed and published by Kolapps ("we", "us"), collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Android app, Wear OS app, and related services. WatchX lets you choose public X/Twitter accounts and view recent public posts from those accounts on your phone and watch.

WatchX is not an official X or Twitter application. WatchX is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or otherwise officially connected to X Corp., Twitter, or their affiliates. X, Twitter, and related names or marks belong to their respective owners. WatchX shows information from publicly available profiles, posts, links, and related public metadata for the accounts you choose.

Android (Google Play): https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kolapps.watchx

iOS (App Store): https://apps.apple.com/us/app/watchx/id6770914400

2. Information We Collect or Process

We do not ask for unrelated personal details. We process the following information to provide and improve WatchX:

  • X/Twitter username or handle. You enter a public username so WatchX can show public posts and profile information for the accounts you select.
  • Selected accounts and public profile data. WatchX stores the list of accounts you choose to follow in the app, along with public profile information such as username, display name, profile image URL, and public account identifiers.
  • Public post content. The app displays and may cache public posts, public profile details, timestamps, links, and engagement metadata returned by our service for the accounts you selected.
  • App-generated user ID and authentication tokens. WatchX creates or receives a stable app user ID and stores access/refresh tokens so the app can securely communicate with our backend without asking you to log in repeatedly.
  • Preferences and settings. We store settings such as language, theme, onboarding state, watch sync preference, premium status, and selected feed configuration.
  • Watch sync data. If you use the Wear OS companion app, your phone may transfer your selected accounts, username, public profile data, theme, language, and premium status to your paired watch so the watch app can work consistently with the phone app.
  • Subscription information. If you buy or restore a subscription, billing is handled by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to validate subscription status and may associate your app user ID and X/Twitter handle with your subscription record.
  • Support information. If you tap Support & Contact, the draft email may include your username and app user ID to help us troubleshoot. You can review and edit the email before sending it.
  • Diagnostics, crash, and usage information. On Android, we use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand app usage, diagnose crashes, and improve reliability. This can include app interactions, device and OS information, crash traces, and identifiers used by Firebase. The Android app declares the Google Advertising ID permission because Firebase/Google Play services may use it for app measurement; WatchX does not show ads.
  • Technical server data. When online features are used, our backend processes request data such as the username, selected creators, authentication token, IP address, user agent, timestamps, and other technical data needed to operate, secure, debug, and protect the service from abuse.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • provide your selected public feed on phone and watch;
  • authenticate requests to our backend and keep your session working;
  • sync settings and selected accounts between your phone and paired watch;
  • cache recent content locally so the app can show recent posts when network access is unavailable;
  • manage subscriptions and premium features;
  • respond to support requests;
  • measure basic app usage, investigate crashes, fix bugs, improve performance, and protect the service.

5. Sharing and Service Providers

We do not sell your personal information. We share or process information only as needed to operate WatchX, including with:

  • RevenueCat for subscription validation and entitlement management;
  • Firebase / Google for Android analytics, crash reporting, diagnostics, and app measurement;
  • Backend hosting and infrastructure providers that host the WatchX API and related services;

6. Local Storage, Cache, and Watch Data

WatchX stores app data locally on your phone and watch, including settings, selected accounts, tokens, subscription state, and cached feed data. This local storage helps the app load faster, remember your choices, and show recent posts when offline. Watch sync data is transferred only between your own paired devices through the platform watch connectivity APIs.
